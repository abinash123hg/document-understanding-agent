# Design

## Visual direction

- Clean and simple document workspace.
- Clear upload and chat interface.
- Responsive layout for mobile and desktop.
- High contrast and readable text.
- Clear buttons and form controls.
- Visible document upload and processing status.

## Responsive requirements

- Use the viewport meta tag.
- Avoid fixed-width layouts.
- Make document and chat areas responsive.
- Support narrow mobile screens.
- Keep buttons and touch targets easy to use.
