COMPANY REMOTE WORK POLICY

Effective Date: January 2025

1. Eligibility
All full-time employees who have completed 3 months of service are eligible for remote work up to 3 days per week.

2. Core Hours
Remote employees must be available online between 10:00 AM and 4:00 PM local time on working days.

3. Equipment Stipend
The company provides a one-time equipment stipend of $600 for home office setup (monitor, chair, keyboard).

4. Leave Policy
Employees receive 18 days of paid annual leave and 12 days of sick leave per calendar year.
Unused annual leave can be carried forward up to a maximum of 5 days.

5. Internet Allowance
A monthly internet allowance of $40 is provided to all remote workers.

6. Meeting Requirements
All team meetings are held on video. Cameras are expected to be on during meetings lasting more than 15 minutes.
